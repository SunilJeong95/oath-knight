01Information we collect

This game does not collect any personal information and does not send any information anywhere.

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting tools.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.

02Information stored on your device

Your game progress (records, achievements, and an unfinished run) and your settings are stored only on your device, in a folder named OathKnight.

AndroidThe app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it, and it is removed when you uninstall the game.
Windows · Steam Deck%APPDATA%\OathKnight\

You can delete this data by uninstalling the game, or with “Reset records” in Settings on the title screen.

On Steam, if Steam Cloud is turned on, Steam keeps these save files in sync with your Steam account. Valve's privacy policy applies to that service.

03Permissions

Vibration (Android)A short vibration when you are hit in battle. You can turn it off with “Vibration” in Settings.

The game requests no other permissions.

04Children’s privacy

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

05Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
